Tier-1 Monocrystalline Architecture

We strictly mandate Bloomberg Tier-1 rated hardware for all microgrid deployments. In the extreme thermal environment of the UAE, module degradation is the primary threat to ROI. Our selected arrays feature anti-PID (Potential Induced Degradation) technology and dual-glass bifacial architecture to maximize albedo capture from reflective roofing surfaces.

Ideal Application High-yield commercial rooftops, logistics hubs, and heavy-draw residential villas.
Thermal Coefficient Engineered to maintain >95% output efficiency at 50°C ambient temperatures.
Lifespan & Warranty 25-Year Linear Power Warranty (Guaranteed >85% output at year 25).
Structural Load Mounting systems rated for 150 km/h wind shear, utilizing aerospace-grade aluminum.

Power Conversion Topologies

The inverter is the brain of the microgrid. We deploy advanced three-phase string inverters for commercial arrays to ensure multiple MPPT (Maximum Power Point Tracking) zones, mitigating the impact of localized shading from HVAC units or parapet walls.

Telemetry Built-in Wi-Fi/4G SCADA connectivity for millisecond-level yield monitoring.
Safety Standards Integrated AFCI (Arc Fault Circuit Interrupter) and Type II SPD on both DC and AC sides.
Grid Sync Phase-locked loop technology for seamless integration with the DEWA municipal grid.

DEWA Net Metering Process.

We act as the principal contractor, handling 100% of the bureaucratic and technical compliance required to inject power back into the Dubai municipal grid under the Shams Dubai initiative.

1. NOC & Structural Approval

We submit CAD layouts, single-line diagrams (SLD), and structural load calculations to DEWA and Dubai Municipality to secure the initial No Objection Certificate.

2. Procurement & Execution

Hardware is delivered and array construction begins. Our in-house engineers ensure all AC/DC cabling meets strict IP65 waterproof and UV-resistant standards.

3. DEWA Inspection & Testing

A DEWA inspector visits the site to verify grounding resistance, breaker sizing, and anti-islanding safety mechanisms before authorizing grid connection.

4. Meter Commissioning

The standard utility meter is replaced with a bi-directional smart meter. The system is activated, and surplus generation begins offsetting your utility bills immediately.
